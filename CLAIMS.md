# Claims & Falsification Ledger (`CLAIMS.md`)

Every headline claim in `pauli-contextuality-lab` is tagged with its exact verification status (`kernel-checked`, `certificate-checked`, `paper proof`, `numerical_approximation`, or `open`), its relation to earlier work, and an explicit falsification condition (Build Standard items B04 & B42).

**Kernel coverage.** `kernel-checked` means checked by the Lean 4.35.0 kernel (`lake build`, `#print axioms` in `lean/VerifyAll.lean`, `lean/VerifyStandalone.lean`, and `lean/VerifyPhaseSeven.lean`, standard axioms only). The **30 comparator targets** in `lean/comparator.json` (Targets 1–30 below, including Track 1 Theorems 23–25, Track 2 actual-context degrees, heterogeneous endpoint reduction, mixed square/pentagram degrees, general-seed universal limit, arbitrary-$k$ $2k$- and $3k$-qubit Pauli realizations, $k$-independent readout-noise thresholds, and kernel-proved false-variant refutations) and their dependency closure are also accepted by 6 independent kernels via `lake comparator --paranoid`. As of `v3.2.0`–`v3.4.0`, the nine standalone Track 1 finite-certificate modules (`Saturated*`, `C3Certificate`, `C3Spectra`, `C3Theorem`, `FlatData`, `PhaseData`) are compiled and axiom-audited in dedicated parallel CI jobs (`lean-verify-standalone` and `lean-verify-phase7`).

**Statement audit.** The wording below was corrected on 2026-11-26 to match an independent statement audit ([audit log](https://github.com/0thernes/0thernes-research-archive/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-11-26-statement-audit.md)); see [ERRATA.md](ERRATA.md).
