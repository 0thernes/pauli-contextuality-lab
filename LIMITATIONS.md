# Limitations, Epistemic Boundaries, and AI-Contribution Disclosure

## 1. Real vs. Metaphor & What Is Not Claimed

- **Not externally peer reviewed:** All three manuscripts in `papers/` are unreviewed research preprints. Passing Lean 4 kernel checks certifies the logical validity of the formalized statements inside Lean — not external referee acceptance, historical priority, or statement-to-literature fidelity.
- **No quantum hardware measurements:** Moonlab (`third_party/moonlab/`) is a classical state-vector simulator compiled to WebAssembly; `quantum_rng` (`third_party/quantum_rng/`) is a seeded classical pseudorandom generator; QGTL (`third_party/qgtl/`) provides classical differential-geometry C kernels. Nothing in this repository is a physical experiment on quantum hardware or certified quantum randomness.
- **Prior work & unreviewed preprint citations:**
  - The spectral floor inequality, its Kramers variant and Conjecture E stem from unreviewed Zenodo preprints by B. W. Jonah (`doi:10.5281/zenodo.23538829`, `23510634`, `23524332`). The floors are Jonah's prior results; this repository formalizes them for arbitrary supports. Jonah's preprint `23538829` also states an attainment criterion for closed families that overlaps `saturation_tfae`. Jonah's computer-assisted note `23510634` (20 October 2026) already claimed the exact values $d(W(7,2)) = 1575$ and $d(Q^+(9,2)) = 6975$, which imply the kernel-checked lower bounds $1455$ and $6444$ proved here. The Conjecture E source is AI-assisted and AI-refereed; it already proves cases C1 and C6.
  - Symplectic polar-space degrees: Muller et al. (`arXiv:2407.02928`). The repeated-disjoint-copy question: A. Muller and M. Saniga (`arXiv:2609.19862v1`, an unreviewed arXiv v1 preprint). Their Sec. 4.2 gives the context-count product formula $|HS(G_1 \cup \dots \cup G_k)| = \prod_i (|HS(G_i)|+1) - 1$ used in Track 2. A. Cabello (`arXiv:1002.3135`, 2010) reports a related growth result, in which the violation grows exponentially with $n$.
  - $d(Q^+(7,2))=315$ is due to Li, Gupta, and Ganesh (`arXiv:2610.26465`); the numerical five-qubit flat state is due to Cieśliński (`arXiv:2610.07172`); the scalar skewness bound is Okumura (1974).
  - Concurrent independent work: a Zenodo deposit (`doi:10.5281/zenodo.24567331`, created 26 November 2026, after the v3.0.0 CI run) describes a floor-type lower bound, the $1/2$ limit, an exactness criterion for pure states with constant-modulus Pauli expectations, a five-qubit configuration of degree 480, and a Lean 4 + Mathlib formalization. It is later than, not prior to, this release; the overlap has not been compared in detail.
  - Novelty claims (Track 1 Section 5 flat states and $|\Psi_{3m}\rangle$, Track 1 Conjecture E formalization, Track 2 arbitrary-$k$ closed forms) rest on bounded literature searches and do not claim exhaustive historical priority. The question answered by $|\Psi_{3m}\rangle$ was posed in this repository's own manuscript, not by another author.
- **Scope of the answers to external questions:**
  - Track 2 answers the stacking-limit question at the end of the second paragraph of Muller & Saniga, Sec. 6, for disjoint Peres–Mermin squares and Mermin pentagrams only. The two doily copies named in the same sentence, mixed unions and the other Sec. 6 outlooks are not treated. The source poses this as an open question in its discussion, not as a conjecture.
  - Track 1 proves strict-law failure for one explicit representative of each of the nine vertex cases of Conjecture E, for every $N \ge 4$. Case membership of each representative and transfer to whole cases (Jonah's Theorem B4) are outside Lean (Section 2).

## 2. Explicit Kernel Boundary (What Lies Outside Lean 4)

The following items are **not** certified by the Lean 4 kernel and are explicitly tagged as `certificate-checked`, `paper proof`, or `numerical_approximation` in `CLAIMS.md`:

1. **42-orbit `W(5,2)` census (`tracks/spectral-bounds/src/w52_census_verify.c`):** Exhaustive C11 enumeration (17,652,160 classes across 42 orbits) + pure-integer Python duals reproducing Jonah's $(315, 1575, 6975)$. Lean proves the exact master reduction (`exact_degrees_n4_n5_of_q7minus_315`) and the lower bounds `1455` and `6444` (which do not depend on the census), not the C orbit search itself. The exact values 1575 and 6975 hold in Lean only conditionally on $d(Q^-(7,2)) \ge 315$.
2. **Nine-qubit cyclic-symmetry rank-8 search (`n9_rank8_search.c`):** C11 enumeration of 540,299,769 forms (0 hits).
3. **Single-orbit transitivity input (`Jonah [J3, Theorem B4]`):** Cited paper input for transporting Conjecture E to arbitrary hyperplane classes outside our constructed representatives. The case labels (from Jonah's list C1–C16) of the nine Lean representatives appear only in comments in `lean/HoggarBound/ConjectureE.lean`; no Lean statement ties a representative to its case.
4. **Floating-point scripts:** Every Python file using `numpy` or floating-point arithmetic is automatically classified in `results/arithmetic-audit.json` (`scripts/arithmetic_audit.py`) and never relied upon for a formal proof.
5. **Physical assumptions of Track 3 (`Evidence`):** The finite adaptive-tree theorem assumes a true conditional setting floor $\beta$ and single context-independent value table per trial; no empirical transcript alone can certify those physical assumptions.
6. **Track 2 identification with the source's objects:** No Lean theorem identifies the hard-coded seed lines in `lean/ContextualityTensor.lean` with $HS(L(K_{3,3}))$ or $HS(\mathrm{Petersen})$; `lean/ContextualityTensor/Support.lean` is generic and not instantiated. The pentagram signs have no Pauli-matrix check in Lean, and `lean/ContextualityTensor/PhysicalBridge.lean` covers two squares only. The identification rests on Python enumeration and the source's Sec. 4.2. The six-kernel comparator targets 9–10 use the tuple-indexed model; the vertex-subset theorems (`global_actual_*`) and the limit theorems (`actual_*_epsilon_tends_to_one`) are checked by the standard Lean kernel only.
7. **Modules not compiled at v3.0.0:** CI and the default Lake targets do not compile `HoggarBound.Saturated`, `SaturatedFive`, `SaturatedPhaseFive`, `SaturatedPhaseSeven`, `C3Certificate`, `C3Spectra`, `C3Theorem`, `FlatData` and `PhaseData`. The finite flat degrees 75, 480 and 729344 and the cospectrality certificate of the C3 sign pair therefore have no compiled, axiom-audited evidence at this release; earlier runs were made in the archived `hoggar-bound-lab` repository.
8. **Six-kernel coverage:** Only the 12 comparator targets in `lean/comparator.json` and their dependency closure were accepted by the 6 independent kernels. All other kernel-checked theorems are checked by the standard Lean kernel. The `leanchecker --fresh` step in CI is time-bounded and report-only, and in CI run 45826042676 it produced no output before the 300 s timeout.
9. **Half-violation limit (`CLAIMS.md` C04):** Lean proves only the lower direction, for $W(2N-1,2)$ and $Q^-(2N-1,2)$; the upper direction and $Q^+(2N-1,2)$ are paper arguments.
10. **Human line-by-line statement fidelity audit:** `formalization.yaml` explicitly records `human_line_by_line_review_recorded: false` until an independent mathematician (or the author line-by-line) signs off on every row of `docs/statement-fidelity.md`.
