## Executive Verdict

- **Grand Mega-Atlas problems completely solved:** **`0 / 1,900`**.
  None of the 1,900 civilizational / grand-challenge open problems in the Mega-Atlas V2 is completely resolved by `pauli-contextuality-lab` or any paper in `0thernes-research-archive`.
- **Finite-dimensional Pauli results checked in Lean 4.35.0 (`pauli-contextuality-lab`):**
  These results are thematically related to Mega-Atlas entry **`QM-011`** (*Quantum Contextuality, Ontological Models & Psi-Ontic vs. Psi-Epistemic Realism*); the atlas entry was not checked to list them as sub-cases. The `QM-011` grand problem (Spekkens generalized contextuality across continuous variables and full resource characterization) stays **OPEN**.
  - *Answers to questions posed by other authors (both sources are unreviewed preprints):*
    1. **Jonah's Conjecture E (Zenodo `doi:10.5281/zenodo.23524332`):** strict-law failure for one explicit representative of each of the 9 vertex cases, every $N \ge 4$ (`HoggarBound.ConjectureE.jonah_conjecture_e_all_nine_cases`, `AuditUpgrades.jonah_conjecture_e_all_nine_cases_spectra`). Jonah already proved cases C1 and C6. Case membership of the representatives and transfer to whole cases (Jonah's Theorem B4) are outside Lean.
    2. **Muller & Saniga (`arXiv:2609.19862v1`, Section 6), stacking-limit question:** exact degrees $d_k = (7^k - 5^k)/2$ for $k$ disjoint Peres–Mermin squares and $(6^k - 4^k)/2$ for $k$ disjoint Mermin pentagrams, with $2d_k/L_k \to 1$, for the explicit union-context family (`ContextualityTensor.square_minimum_closed_form`, `pentagram_minimum_closed_form`, `global_actual_*`). Squares and pentagrams only; identification with the source's $HS(kG)$ is by enumeration, outside Lean.
  - *New theorem (question posed in this repository's own manuscript):*
    3. **Infinite family of non-closed pure-CCZ Pauli sets $|\Psi_{3m}\rangle$ attaining the spectral floor** for all $m \ge 1$, contextual for $m \ge 2$ (`HoggarBound.NormPhaseFamily.infinite_nonclosed_saturated_family`), with degree `2016` at $m = 2$. The finite degrees `75`, `480`, `729344` are in modules not compiled by CI at `v3.0.0`.
  - *Bounded instance and lower bounds:*
    - Exact binary completion optima `368` and `344` for one four-qubit Case C3 sign pair (`C3ExactOptima.corollary16_exact_binary_optima`), a single finite instance.
    - Kernel-checked lower bounds $d(W(7,2)) \ge 1455$ and $d(Q^+(9,2)) \ge 6444$; Jonah (`doi:10.5281/zenodo.23510634`) earlier claimed the exact values `1575` and `6975`.
  - *Formalizations of known or elementary results:*
    4. **Jonah's universal and Kramers spectral floors** (prior work, here for arbitrary supports) and the **Peres–Mermin setting-bias floor & finite betting-tree hitting bound** (`Evidence.sharp_minimum`, `BetTree.finite_hitting_bound`, `concrete_classical_false_positive`), which `papers/sequential-evidence/PRIOR_ART.md` describes as elementary consequences of their assumptions.
